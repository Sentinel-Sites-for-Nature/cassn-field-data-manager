# CSU stacked wordmark

Official source: CSU Brand Guidelines, updated August 2018, page 1.
https://www.calstate.edu/csu-system/csu-branding-standards/Documents/CSU-Brand-Guidelines-8-2018.pdf

The SVG preserves the official stacked wordmark's vector paths and original
colors, extracted with Poppler. Its viewBox includes clear space of at least
half the CSU block's height on all sides. A white backing preserves legibility
in both light and dark app appearances. No lettering was recreated or recolored.

The original small PNG requested by John is also preserved, unmodified:
https://www.calstate.edu/csu-system/csu-branding-standards/PublishingImages/csu-wordmark-stacked.png

The app uses the vector version so it remains crisp on Retina displays and when
the user zooms the interface. CSU retains ownership of its wordmark.
